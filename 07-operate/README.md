# Operations and reliability

A launched product needs observability, support, backups, recovery, incident response, capacity planning, data operations, change management and continuous improvement.

Cover logs, metrics, traces, user-visible service indicators, SLOs, alert quality, runbooks, on-call expectations, postmortems, RTO/RPO, restore drills, support queues, status pages and changelogs. A backup is not proven until restoration is tested.
