# Education product discovery and design

**Maturity:** reviewed domain guide; no learning product or student-data processing has been evaluated.

Education products serve learners, educators, administrators, parents, institutions, and sometimes employers. The purchaser, user, and person whose data is collected may differ. Start with a learning or administrative task and state the intended educational outcome.

## Protect learning and student agency

Map who sets goals, creates or reviews work, sees progress, and can correct records. Ask educators what a successful outcome looks like, how learning is assessed now, and which accommodations or language needs matter. Avoid optimizing for time-on-platform, clicks, or automated scores if they do not represent learning.

For a first aid, use teacher-authored content and a tool that organizes practice, gives transparent hints, or reduces admin work. Keep assessment decisions with qualified educators; give learners a way to understand and challenge feedback. Avoid collecting sensitive learner profiles unless the educational purpose and safeguards are clear.

## Privacy, age, accessibility, and integrity

- Inventory learner data, purpose, school/parent authorization, processors, retention, and deletion.
- Apply age-appropriate defaults and avoid behavioral advertising or unnecessary tracking.
- Provide accessible content, keyboard and screen-reader support, and alternatives to timed/visual interaction.
- Make AI use visible; cite learning material, distinguish hints from verified answers, and support educator review.
- Test bias and accessibility with representative participants and permitted data.
- Maintain a human escalation and correction path for safety or student-record issues.

In the U.S., the Department of Education's [student privacy resource center](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/resources) provides FERPA guidance, including online educational service resources. FERPA applicability depends on the institution, records, and role; COPPA and state/local laws may also matter. Other jurisdictions have separate rules. Obtain school/privacy review before handling real student records.

Measure completion of the target learning task, quality of learner explanations, educator time saved, corrections, accessibility barriers, and unintended differences among groups. Use a study design suitable for the claim; an engagement increase does not prove learning improvement.
