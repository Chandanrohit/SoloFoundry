# Business and commercial operations

Cover business models, pricing research, packaging, billing, payment operations, unit economics, cash flow, runway, taxes and accounting concepts, contracts, IP, privacy notices, terms, customer support and vendor management.

Legal and tax requirements vary by jurisdiction; use qualified local professionals for decisions with legal or financial consequences. Explain concepts without pretending a generic guide replaces professional advice.

## Learning path

1. [Pricing and unit economics](pricing-and-unit-economics/README.md): test an offer and understand contribution and break-even assumptions.
2. [Cash flow and financial controls](cashflow-and-financial-controls/README.md): track actual cash timing, obligations, and scenarios.
3. [Legal, privacy, contracts, and support](legal-privacy-contracts-and-support/README.md): map obligations, align public terms with practice, and build a support process.
4. Use the [pricing model worksheet](../templates/PRICING_MODEL.md) and connect acquisition tests to [growth experiments](../templates/GROWTH_EXPERIMENT.md).

These guides explain general concepts, not jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, or accounting advice. No business model or price has been validated with actual customers.
