## Interpreting status

- **Reviewed** means an editorial pass checked clarity, internal consistency, and source framing. It does not mean the method is experimentally validated or that it establishes market demand.
- **Draft** means material is incomplete or has not received the defined review.
- **Planned** means a topic map or future deliverable exists, not a usable guide.
- The worked example in `01-discover/worked-example/` is hypothetical and must not be used as empirical evidence.
- Versioned, legal, tax, regulatory, and market claims need dated source checks before readers rely on them.

Update this file whenever a guide changes maturity. Phase 2 guide-level review is an editorial synthesis; no specific product or prototype has been tested. Phase 3 guides are also method syntheses, not tested implementations. Phases 4 and 5 are practical method guides with exercises and decision frameworks; no model integration, deployment, security assessment, or restore drill has been executed. Independent review remains open for all phases.
