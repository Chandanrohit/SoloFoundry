# Growth and distribution

Growth starts with a clear audience, promise and credible path to reach users. Cover positioning, messaging, launch planning, content, SEO/GEO, communities, partnerships, outbound, product-led growth, onboarding, activation, retention, referrals, analytics and experimentation.

Do not confuse traffic with demand or signups with retained value. Tie each channel to a target segment, cost, conversion, payback period and learning objective.
