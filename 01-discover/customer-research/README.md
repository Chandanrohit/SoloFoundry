# Customer research

Interview for past behavior, not hypothetical enthusiasm. Ask the person to walk through the last time the problem occurred: trigger, steps, tools, people involved, time, cost, failure points and workaround.

## Useful questions
- “Tell me about the last time you had to do this.”
- “What happened immediately before and after?”
- “What did you try? What did it cost in time or money?”
- “How often does this happen? Who else is involved?”
- “What happens if nothing changes?”
- “Have you paid for or built a workaround?”

Avoid leading questions such as “Would you use my app?” Ask permission before recording. Protect personal and commercially sensitive information.
