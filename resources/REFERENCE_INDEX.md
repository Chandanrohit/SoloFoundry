# Reference index

These are starting points for research, not endorsements or a substitute for evaluating current documentation and licenses.

## Product discovery
- [Product Discovery Toolkit](https://github.com/saurabh1chawda/product-discovery-toolkit) — discovery canvases, interview guides, evidence ladder and validation scorecards.

## SaaS lifecycle
- [SaaS Playbook](https://github.com/monooleate/saas-playbook) — stack-agnostic SaaS implementation topics, production gotchas and checklists.
- [SaaS Launch Checklist](https://github.com/saas-playbooks/saas-launch-checklist) — architecture through production launch checklist.

## Engineering
- [Engineering Playbook](https://github.com/pato-byepaper/engineering-playbook) — broad engineering, architecture, AI, cloud and security handbook.
- [Engineering Handbook](https://github.com/handbook-academy/engineering-handbook) — system design, AI engineering and security learning material.

## Business and growth
- [U.S. Small Business Administration: plan your business](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/) — market research, competitive analysis and startup-cost resources; U.S.-specific.
- [U.S. Small Business Administration: manage your business](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/manage-your-business/) — bookkeeping, finance, and compliance starting points; U.S.-specific.
- [Federal Trade Commission: advertising and marketing](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing) — U.S. truth-in-advertising and endorsement guidance.
- [Google Search Central: Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) and [generative AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) — Google-specific discovery requirements and guidance, not a promise of ranking or traffic.
- [Your Europe: data protection under GDPR](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/governance-and-sustainability/digital-and-data-compliance/data-protection-gdpr/index_en.htm) — EU business overview; assess applicability and current local rules.
