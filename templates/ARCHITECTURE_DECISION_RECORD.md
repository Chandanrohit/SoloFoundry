- **Status:** proposed / accepted / superseded

## Context
Requirements, constraints, scale, threat model and operational context.

## Decision drivers
Rank the properties that matter: cost, latency, availability, privacy, team skill, delivery speed, portability, maintainability.

## Options considered
Include a simpler option and the option of not building.

## Decision
State the chosen approach and assumptions.

## Consequences
Benefits, trade-offs, risks, operational work and migration implications.

## Review trigger
What evidence or scale change would cause this decision to be revisited?
