# Customer interview guide

## Before the interview
- Define what you need to learn; do not pitch.
- Recruit people who recently experienced the workflow.
- Explain purpose, consent, recording and data handling.

## Opening
“I'm researching how people currently handle [workflow]. I'm not selling anything; I'm interested in what actually happens.”

## Questions
1. Walk me through the last time you did this.
2. What triggered it?
3. What steps and tools did you use?
4. Where did it become slow, expensive, risky or frustrating?
5. How often does it happen?
6. What happens when it goes wrong?
7. What workaround have you tried?
8. Have you spent money or assigned people to solve it?
9. Who decides whether a change can be adopted?
10. What did I fail to ask?

## After the interview
Capture direct observations separately from interpretation. Note contradictions, follow-up evidence and changes to assumptions.
