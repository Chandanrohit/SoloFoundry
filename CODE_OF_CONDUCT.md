# Community code of conduct

SoloFoundry welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and ways of building. We want practical disagreement about ideas while keeping the people discussing them safe and respected.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Critique claims, designs, or evidence without attacking people.
- Credit sources and contributors; distinguish fact, assumption, and opinion.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data or correspondence without permission.
- Make space for accessibility needs and different levels of expertise.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, repeated bad-faith disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

Contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile with a concise description and links. For immediate safety concerns, use the relevant platform reporting tools. Maintainers may remove content, edit or close discussions, or restrict participation based on impact and context. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
